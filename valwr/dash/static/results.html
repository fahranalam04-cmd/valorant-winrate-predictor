Every match the dashboard saw is recorded here, scored against the real result once it is published. Only standard bomb-defusal matches count toward the figures: the model never saw swiftplay or deathmatch, so folding those in would measure the wrong thing.
The record of what was predicted is never a feature or a label. The matches themselves are stored like any other and reach training through the time-ordered split; tools/improve.py reports which of them have moved inside the training window and can no longer judge the model.
